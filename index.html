About Neondesk Africa

Neondesk Africa is a business support company with one mission: to grow African entrepreneurs and their businesses.

What started as a platform to connect Nigerian entrepreneurs with mentors and education has evolved into a full business support company. We have two focused divisions — Neondesk Studio builds and launches proprietary programs and products that equip African entrepreneurs with the tools they need, and Neondesk Lab works directly with established businesses to build growth systems, get more customers, and scale revenue.

We operate across Africa because that's where the work matters most. We believe African entrepreneurs don't just deserve access to capital — they deserve access to the knowledge, systems, and networks that make businesses actually grow.

The Problem

The challenges African entrepreneurs face.

These are the real, recurring problems we see every day across Nigeria and Africa. If any of these sound familiar — you're exactly who we built Neondesk for.

01

You have a great idea but no clear path to build it.

Ambition without direction burns out fast. Most entrepreneurs know what they want — not how to build it into something that lasts.

02

Your business growth is inconsistent.

Good month today, bad month tomorrow. No predictable system to understand what's driving results or how to repeat them.

03

Customer acquisition is expensive and unclear.

You're spending money to get customers but it's not showing in revenue. You can't tell what's actually working.

04

You have no access to the right networks.

In Africa, who you know still opens doors. Without access to the right investors, mentors, and partners — growth stalls.

05

You have campaigns. Not a system.

When something works, you can't repeat it. When it fails, you don't know why. That's not a business strategy — that's guessing.

06

Business education doesn't reflect African realities.

Most programs recycle Western frameworks that don't translate. You need knowledge built for this market — not borrowed from another one.

How We Solve It

Here's what we actually do.

We don't give generic advice from a distance. We go into the work — with proper systems, accountability, and context built specifically for Africa.

01

We diagnose exactly what's holding your business back.

Before we prescribe anything, we understand your business — your model, your market, your customers, and where growth is leaking out.

02

We build systems to fix it — and keep it fixed.

A proper growth engine that runs consistently, tracks what matters, and tells you exactly what's driving results so you can repeat it.

03

We get you more quality customers.

We find where your customers are, how to reach them efficiently, and how to acquire them at a cost that makes your business profitable.

04

We equip entrepreneurs with Africa-relevant knowledge.

Through Neondesk Studio, we deliver education that speaks to African market realities — not recycled frameworks built for another context.

05

We connect you to the people who move businesses forward.

Investors, mentors, collaborators, and partners. We open the right doors through our growing network of operators and leaders across Africa.

06

We know Nigeria. We understand Africa.

We've grown businesses here. We understand what actually works in Lagos, Abuja, Accra, Nairobi — and we bring that context to every engagement.

Built for African entrepreneurs

Neondesk has two divisions for a reason — we serve early-stage entrepreneurs building from scratch and established businesses ready to scale. Here's how to know if you're a fit.

You're a fit if:
  • ✓You're a student, graduate, NYSC member, or young professional with the ambition to build something real.
  • ✓You're an early-stage founder who has started but needs structure, accountability, and a clear path forward.
  • ✓You're an established business generating revenue and ready to grow it consistently and sustainably.
  • ✓You want more than generic advice — you need a system that actually works in the African market.
  • ✓You believe in community — in learning from and pushing others on the same journey as you.
  • ✓You're ready to invest in your business — time, discipline, and the right resources to do things properly.
You're NOT a fit if:
  • ✗You're looking for a shortcut, a get-rich-quick scheme, or results without putting in real work.
  • ✗You're not open to honest feedback or willing to challenge how you currently run your business.
  • ✗You want to "just run some ads" without building systems that make those ads actually sustainable.
  • ✗You're not ready to commit — short-term thinking breaks long-term businesses.
  • ✗You want someone to take all the responsibility while you stay uninvolved in your own growth.

Questions

Neondesk Studio is our builder division — it creates the programs, courses, tools, and community that entrepreneurs need to start and grow a business. Think of it as the product we've built for the market. Neondesk Lab is our growth division — it works directly with established businesses to diagnose growth problems, build customer acquisition systems, and scale revenue. Studio is for entrepreneurs who are learning and building. Lab is for businesses that are already running and need to grow faster and more consistently.

Both — and that's intentional. Neondesk Studio handles the education and community side: programs, mentorship, and a structured framework for aspiring entrepreneurs. Neondesk Lab handles the hands-on business growth side: strategy, customer acquisition, and revenue systems for established businesses. Most companies do one or the other. We do both because African entrepreneurs need both — at different stages of their journey.

We work across industries — food and hospitality, technology, fintech, retail, professional services, and more. The principles of building and growing a business are largely universal. What changes is how you apply them in specific African markets. We've worked with telecoms, financial institutions, lifestyle brands, and tech startups — and we bring direct experience from each of these sectors to every engagement.

No. Neondesk Africa is a private business. We believe the best thing we can do for African entrepreneurship is build a sustainable company that shows up and delivers results — not one that depends on donor funding to survive. We charge for our services because that's what allows us to remain committed, accountable, and excellent in everything we do.

For Studio members, the impact starts immediately — access to knowledge, community, and accountability from day one. For Lab clients, early signals typically appear within the first 60–90 days as systems are put in place, with significant and measurable results building over 6 months. We will always be honest about timelines and will never overpromise what's not possible in your specific context.

For Studio: you join a membership plan, get access to our resource library, live sessions, expert Q&As, and a peer community. For Lab clients: we begin with a full growth diagnostic of your business — how you acquire customers, where revenue is leaking, why growth is inconsistent. We then build a custom roadmap and work alongside your team to execute it, with regular check-ins, reporting, and accountability throughout. We are in the business with you, not just advising from a distance.
